Transparent Mathematical Formulation

Every post in your feed is assigned a composite score from 0 to 100 using strictly enforced normalized weights.

Final Score = (Recency × WR) + (Engagement × WE) + (Affinity × WA)

Ensures smooth, continuous decay without abrupt cliff effects.

Log-Compressed Virality S_eng = (ln(1 + Raw) / ln(1 + Max)) · 100
